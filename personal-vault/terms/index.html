Your vault and your content

Personal Vault is provided by Cross Trading LLC. It stores content on your device; use it only for content you have the right to store. You retain your rights to your content. Local encryption and app locking do not guarantee protection on a compromised device or for copies outside the app.

Keep a recoverable copy

Keep a verified encrypted backup outside the app and its passphrase separately. Forgotten credentials cannot be reset by support. Losing all usable unlock methods, the device key or the backup passphrase can make your collection inaccessible. Restore into a fresh installation and verify recovery before deleting your only working copy. Do not uninstall or clear a real vault to test recovery.

Imports, exports and removal

Imports keep originals unless you select supported photo/video removal after verified import. Your operating system may request confirmation; synced libraries and Recently Deleted can retain or propagate changes. General files require manual source removal. Check exported copies in the destination app. Recently removed retains encrypted content and uses device storage; it is reversible and has no automatic purge. Any future permanent deletion will require explicit confirmation.

Selected subscription direction

Personal Vault Pro is planned as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription with the same benefits. The selected US reference prices are $5.99 per month or $39.99 per year, charged upfront for the year. These are preparation references, not an offer to purchase here. The app and native store confirmation must display the full localized price, billing period, paid service and renewal terms before you confirm. No free trial, prepaid plan, installment plan or hosted cloud storage is selected.

The selected free allowance is 25 active private items across photos, videos, files and notes, with new imports up to 25 MiB each. Recently removed items do not use active-item slots, but still occupy device storage. Recovery must remain available even above the free allowance. Free includes supported unlock methods, encrypted backup and restore, scanning and private text search. Current test builds do not enforce a customer item-count limit.

Planned Pro removes the active-item count cap and adds seven functional tool covers with platform-specific icon and naming options. Device storage still limits capacity. The prepared larger-file target is 250 MiB per file for supported imports after device acceptance; it is not an activated customer benefit. Notes and some operations retain lower limits shown in the app. The actual version offered must state its delivered limits. Everyday cover notes, tasks, plans and laps are visible without vault authentication and are separate from private vault content and backups. On iPhone, alternate icons do not rename the installed app; a user-created Shortcut can have a custom Home Screen name.

When a subscription is offered, it renews automatically under the purchasing store’s displayed terms unless you cancel renewal. Manage or cancel through the purchasing store. Cancelling renewal does not itself end the current paid period. Refunds and purchase issues follow the purchasing store’s rules and applicable rights. Expiry, refunds or billing problems never delete, re-encrypt or lock existing vault content. Existing items remain readable, editable, restorable and exportable; new additions may follow the free allowance. The chosen appearance and everyday cover data are not automatically removed on expiry, and restoring the Personal Vault appearance stays free.

Restoring purchases restores subscription access, not vault files. Purchases are not promised to transfer between Apple and Google accounts. A restore can associate a plan with the current installation under the purchasing store and provider rules; native reinstall and second-device behavior still need acceptance.

App license and third-party services

For an iPhone App Store release using Apple's Standard EULA, the Apple Standard EULA governs the application license. Final publisher review must confirm the selected license arrangement. System pickers, photo libraries, export destinations, scanning components and purchase services have their own applicable terms. No cloud vault hosting is included.

Updates and statutory rights

Supported features, device requirements and prices will be described for the version actually offered. Substantive subscription changes must follow the store's disclosure and consent processes. This proposal does not exclude rights that applicable law makes mandatory. Final license, liability and dispute provisions require publisher review before publication.
