Your vault and your content

Personal Vault is provided by Cross Trading LLC. It stores content on your device; use it only for content you have the right to store. You retain your rights to your content. Local encryption and app locking do not guarantee protection on a compromised device or for copies outside the app.

Keep a recoverable copy

Keep a verified encrypted backup outside the app and its passphrase separately. Forgotten credentials cannot be reset by support. Losing all usable unlock methods, the device key or the backup passphrase can make your collection inaccessible. Restore into a fresh installation and verify recovery before deleting your only working copy. Do not uninstall or clear a real vault to test recovery.

Imports, exports and removal

Imports keep originals unless you select supported photo/video removal after verified import. Your operating system may request confirmation; synced libraries and Recently Deleted can retain or propagate changes. General files require manual source removal. Check exported copies in the destination app. Recently removed retains encrypted content and uses device storage; it is reversible and has no automatic purge. Any future permanent deletion will require explicit confirmation.

Subscriptions

Purchases are currently disabled. Final plans, prices and benefits are undecided. If subscriptions are offered after launch, the app and native store confirmation will display the full localized price, billing period, paid service and automatic-renewal terms before you confirm. Trials, prepaid and installment products are not part of the prepared purchase flow.

Manage or cancel renewal through the purchasing store. Cancelling renewal does not itself end an active paid period. Refunds and purchase issues are handled under the purchasing store's rules and applicable rights. Expiry or billing problems must never delete or lock existing vault content; future additions may follow the applicable free allowance. Restoring purchases restores subscription access, not your vault files. Purchases are not promised to transfer between Apple and Google accounts.

App license and third-party services

For an iPhone App Store release using Apple's Standard EULA, the Apple Standard EULA governs the application license. Final publisher review must confirm the selected license arrangement. System pickers, photo libraries, export destinations, scanning components and purchase services have their own applicable terms. No cloud vault hosting is included.

Updates and statutory rights

Supported features, device requirements and prices will be described for the version actually offered. Substantive subscription changes must follow the store's disclosure and consent processes. This proposal does not exclude rights that applicable law makes mandatory. Final license, liability and dispute provisions require publisher review before publication.
