Terms proposal

Your private collection

Personal Vault is developed by Cross Trading LLC. The app stores photos, videos and files you select, and notes you create, on your device. Saved file contents, note bodies, filenames and collection information are encrypted. Imported files may include embedded information, such as photo metadata.

The app does not provide an online vault account, advertising or a cloud vault backend that receives your private collection. Document scanning includes Google components on Android; proposed billing uses RevenueCat. Their distinct processing is described below. Final installed components and network behavior still require verification before release.

PIN, password, pattern and biometrics

A six-digit PIN and a device-held secret protect access to your collection. You may add a password, a pattern, or supported secure biometric unlock. The app uses your PIN, password or pattern to protect encryption keys; it does not save those credentials as plain text.

Face and fingerprint authentication is handled by your operating system. Personal Vault does not receive your biometric templates. When you enable biometric unlock, a separate unlock token is protected using the device’s secure storage and authentication. Changes to enrolled biometrics may require using another enabled method.

The app locks when it enters the background. No protection can guarantee the privacy of screenshots, a compromised device, your keyboard or copies you deliberately export.

Imports, permissions and exports

System pickers let you choose photos, videos and files. Imports normally copy the selection and keep the original. The picker or provider may download an item from a service you already use. Personal Vault does not provide its own cloud storage.

If you choose Move photos, the app saves and verifies an encrypted copy before requesting removal of supported originals from your photo library. This requires library access and may require your device’s confirmation. Removal can affect a synced photo library. Originals may remain in Recently Deleted; Personal Vault does not empty that album.

Unavailable source identifiers, permission restrictions and unsupported compound media can prevent automatic removal. The import verifies the selected copy, not preservation of all source editing history or metadata. Files selected from other applications require manual source removal after you check the vault copy.

Move out deliberately saves an outside copy to Photos or your chosen export destination. That copy is subject to the receiving app or service’s settings and policies. Your encrypted vault copy remains until you explicitly remove it. For file exports, share-sheet completion does not establish that the destination saved the file; check it before confirming removal.

The app requests photo-library access for these transfer features and biometric permission where required. iPhone document scanning requests camera permission. Android document scanning uses the Google Play services scanner. The optional QR cover requests app camera permission on iPhone and Android only when you start scanning. Microphone and precise media-location access are not requested for these workflows. Imported files can still contain their own metadata.

Everyday tool covers

The planned Pro covers include Calculator, Notebook, Unit Converter, To-do List, Stopwatch, Daily Planner and QR Scanner. Everyday cover notes, tasks, plans and saved laps are visible without vault authentication. Use the private vault for sensitive content. Saved everyday data uses an independent device key; this does not add a vault lock to the covers. It does not count toward the private-item allowance and is not included in vault backups, automatic sync or a recovery service. Save edits explicitly; abrupt process termination can lose unsaved changes. Removed notes/tasks/plans stay until restored or permanently deleted after confirmation. Reinstall, clearing data or key loss may make them inaccessible. Stopwatch timing uses the device clock; Planner has no reminders/calendar integration.

QR scanning starts only when you choose and needs camera permission. It saves no photos, scan history or QR content. Results clear on background or vault entry. Copying is explicit and places text on the system clipboard, which other apps or OS history may retain. The tool never reads your clipboard. Only HTTP/HTTPS websites can be opened after separate confirmation; the outside app/site then applies its own policy. No automatic Wi-Fi setup or other QR actions occur. Native acceptance is pending.

Storage, recovery and temporary copies

Saved content remains in local storage while you use the app. Recently removed is reversible and does not permanently delete encrypted records. Permanent per-item deletion of private vault items is not available in this version. The separate everyday Notebook offers explicitly confirmed permanent deletion.

You may create a portable encrypted backup with a separate passphrase and save it to a destination you choose. Backups include notes, scan text, albums and recently removed items. A backup can restore into an uninitialized vault with fresh device credentials; it cannot replace an existing vault. You can change a known PIN in Settings. There is no forgotten-PIN reset, passphrase recovery or automatic cloud sync. Without a verified backup and its passphrase, uninstalling, clearing app data or losing the device key may make content inaccessible. The chosen backup destination and operating-system backups follow their own settings and policies.

Viewing and exporting can create temporary unencrypted files in the app’s cache. The app attempts to remove these after use, when locking and on startup. Crashes or interrupted operations can leave temporary files until later cleanup or operating-system eviction.

Notes must be saved before leaving the editor. Unsaved edits are discarded when you close the editor or lock the vault.

Optional store ratings

The planned rating feature keeps limited usage counts and request timestamps on this device to avoid asking too often. It does not read private vault content or send those counters to an app analytics service. If you choose to submit a store rating or review, the app store processes it under its own account and privacy rules; reviews may be public. The app does not receive your selected stars or review text through this feature and does not reward or restrict features based on a rating. Rating activation and native SDK/privacy verification are still pending.

This website and support email

These proposed Personal Vault pages contain no analytics code, advertising code, forms or third-party embedded assets. They do not upload or open your vault content. This description applies to these pages, not to every service or page you may visit through a link.

The existing company website uses GitHub Pages. Website hosting is separate from the app’s on-device storage. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and visit information, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The current hosting configuration and any additional website services must be reviewed before the policy becomes effective.

If you email contact@crosstrading.llc, your email provider and the recipient’s email service process the message and sender information. The company receives information you choose to include for support. Do not send private vault content, your PIN, password or pattern.

Google hosts the support mailbox, and the publisher can read support messages. The approved support rule is to retain routine messages for 90 days after resolution, then remove them from the active mailbox. Records may be kept longer only for a documented legal requirement or unresolved dispute. This rule concerns support correspondence, not vault content. Provider Trash, backups and any legal retention can have separate lifecycles; it is not a promise of complete physical erasure on day 90. Mailbox delivery, additional access and the actual retention workflow still require verification; no automatic mailbox deletion has been configured by this revision.

Document scans and private search

Scanned JPEG pages are encrypted when imported into the vault. Recognized text and note search indexes are stored inside the encrypted catalog. Recognition is performed on-device using Apple Vision on iPhone and bundled Latin text recognition from Google ML Kit on Android. Recognition may be incomplete or inaccurate. Pending scan pages are temporary plaintext in the app-private cache until imported or cleaned up; operating-system and scanner-framework copies are outside the vault’s control.

Google states that ML Kit does not send input images or recognized output to its servers, but can contact Google for updates and sends performance and usage metrics. Android scanner components may download through Google Play services before first use. See Google ML Kit privacy information. Google’s general disclosure guidance also describes device and app metadata, installation identifiers and timing/error events used for diagnostics and usage analysis. See ML Kit’s data-disclosure guide. The guide covers current SDK releases; final installed-version behavior and store disclosures still require review.

Subscriptions and purchase records

Apple subscription drafts and a RevenueCat Pro offering are prepared. App billing remains disabled; native purchase testing is pending. Preparing these records does not charge you or make a plan available for sale.

When billing is enabled, Apple or Google handles payment, and RevenueCat validates purchases and maintains subscription status. This can involve store receipts or purchase tokens, product and transaction information, renewal and entitlement status, a generated anonymous app-user identifier, and technical information such as device type, operating system, locale and currency. The SDK can request subscription status even if you have not bought a plan. Purchase records support app functionality, purchase analysis and fraud prevention. Vault contents, filenames, encryption keys and unlock credentials are not passed to the billing adapter. Personal Vault does not receive your payment-card details.

The current source does not pass an email address or custom identified account to RevenueCat and disables automatic advertising-identifier collection. This does not mean that purchase processing is anonymous in every legal sense, or that the app has no network activity. Final installed SDK behavior and store labels still require verification.

The 90-day support-email rule does not apply to Apple, Google or RevenueCat transaction records. Provider processing, retention and any legal or dispute needs must be reconciled with the final service arrangement; uninstalling the app or cancelling renewal is not a request to erase those records. For questions or requests about information handled through Personal Vault, contact contact@crosstrading.llc. See RevenueCat’s privacy information. Support cannot decrypt or recover your local vault.

Subscription expiry, refunds and billing problems do not change vault keys or delete existing content. Existing items remain readable, editable, restorable and exportable. New additions may follow the applicable free allowance after Pro ends. Restoring purchases restores a plan, not vault files; a local vault backup is a separate operation.

Usage and beta diagnostics

The current app code has no advertising or separate app-usage analytics service. RevenueCat purchase analysis and Android scanner metrics are separate processing described on this page. Optional rating counters stay on the device. No zero-network claim is made for the complete app.

Prepared beta diagnostics keep native encryption-check status, the selected derivation engine, fixed fallback categories and unlock timings in memory on the device. They reset when the app process restarts and are not uploaded by this feature. They do not retain PINs, passwords, patterns, keys, filenames, file content, identifiers or raw errors. If you choose to email a screenshot or timings for support, that message follows the support-email rule.

Optional app appearance

The prepared mobile feature offers alternate icons and preset Android launcher names. On iPhone, a custom Home Screen name uses an Apple Shortcut created by the user. The installed app can still appear in system settings, the App Library or app drawer, and purchase history. These cosmetic choices do not change encryption or unlock methods and do not send vault content to a service. New alternate selections are a proposed Pro benefit; resetting the default is free, and expiry never automatically changes the selected appearance. Native behavior still requires acceptance. This is a review proposal; final native acceptance is pending.
