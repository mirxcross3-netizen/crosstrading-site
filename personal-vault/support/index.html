Prelaunch guide

A little help
with your vault.

Review revision: November 10, 2026. Native purchase and final device acceptance are pending.

These answers describe the current development version. Personal Vault is not yet available in the app stores.

How do I add photos, videos, files or notes?

Create a six-digit PIN when you first open the app. Tap Add and choose Photos & videos, Import files or Write a note. Photos and videos can be selected together. Each file must be non-empty and no larger than 25 MiB.

Imports use the copy provided by the system picker. A picker may download a selection from your existing file or photo provider. That does not create a Personal Vault cloud backup.

Will importing remove the original?

Copy keeps the original. If you choose Move photos, Personal Vault saves and verifies an encrypted copy before requesting removal from your photo library. Your device may ask you to approve the removal.

Limited permissions, unavailable library identifiers and some compound media can prevent removal. Live Photos and media with unsupported motion or depth information are kept in Photos. Files selected from other apps must be removed there manually after you check the vault copy.

Photo-library removal may also affect synced devices or cloud libraries. Items may remain in the system’s Recently Deleted album; Personal Vault does not empty it. The import does not promise preservation of every source edit or metadata field.

How do I move something back out?
  1. Open the item and select Move out.
  2. Save photos or videos to your photo library, or export to your chosen file destination.
  3. Check that the outside copy is saved and opens correctly.
  4. Choose whether to keep the vault copy or remove it.

The vault copy is kept until you explicitly remove it. For files, finishing the share sheet does not prove that a destination saved the file, so check the copy yourself before confirming.

Which unlock methods can I use?

Every vault starts with a six-digit PIN. Under Settings → Unlock methods, enter your current PIN to add a password, a pattern, or supported device biometrics. Passwords use 10–128 characters. Patterns connect or select 6–9 different dots.

Face ID, Touch ID or Android face/fingerprint availability depends on the device. Only supported strong biometrics can be enabled; some Android face-unlock systems do not qualify. The PIN remains available as a fallback. Changing enrolled biometrics may require another unlock method.

What if I forget my PIN?

Cross Trading LLC cannot retrieve or reset your PIN. If another enrolled method still works, unlock your vault and export important content. The current PIN is required to change optional unlock methods.

Do not uninstall the app or clear its data as a troubleshooting step. There is no automatic cloud recovery. A previously saved encrypted backup and its passphrase can restore your collection into a fresh vault on another device. Losing all working unlock methods or the device key without such a backup may make saved content inaccessible.

Why did the app lock during an import?

Personal Vault locks when it moves into the background. A system picker can cause this. Unlock again to let a pending import continue. If a removal or save request was interrupted, check the photo library or destination before trying again. Your vault copy is not automatically removed because a share sheet closed.

Can I recover an item I removed from the vault?

Open Settings → Recently removed, open the item and choose Restore. It returns to the vault. Recovery remains available above the selected free allowance. Recently removed does not use active-item slots, but still uses device storage. This is separate from the phone’s own Recently Deleted album.

Recently removed is reversible and is not automatically emptied. Permanent per-item deletion is not available in this version, so moving items there does not free their encrypted storage.

Are notes saved automatically?

No. Tap Save note before closing the editor or leaving the app. Unsaved edits are discarded when you close or lock the vault. Saved notes can be edited, organized in albums and exported as text files.

How do encrypted backups work?

Open Settings → Encrypted backup. Choose and confirm a separate 12–128 character passphrase. The app prepares and verifies an encrypted .pvbackup file; then choose Save encrypted backup. Check that the file exists in the destination you selected. Store its passphrase separately; it cannot be recovered.

On a fresh installation, choose Restore vault backup before creating a vault. Select the backup, enter its passphrase and choose a new six-digit PIN. Restore never replaces an existing vault. Set up optional unlock methods again afterward. New imports and notes remain limited to 25 MiB. Compatible backups may restore larger existing files up to 250 MiB each; recovery does not increase the limit for new imports. This larger-file recovery path still needs installed-phone acceptance before launch. Backups are manual; there is no automatic cloud sync.

Can I scan and search documents?

On a supported phone, choose Add → Scan document. Capture up to 10 pages, which are saved as JPEG images. Recognition supports English/Latin text; it may miss or misread words. Search matches filenames, saved note text and recognized scan text. The search index is limited to the first 65,536 text units per item and indicates truncation.

Android scanning requires Google Play services and may need an initial component download. If the app locks during scanning, unlock to continue importing. Pending scan pages are temporary until the app confirms encrypted import. If text recognition fails, the image can still be saved without searchable text. Browser preview does not capture documents.

Can I subscribe now?

No. Personal Vault is still in development. Apple subscription drafts and the RevenueCat Pro offering are prepared, but app billing is disabled and actual native purchase testing is pending. Prices will come from your store when products and launch terms are approved. Backup, search and unlock methods remain available without a subscription; selected US Pro pricing is $5.99/month or $39.99/year (the year is charged upfront), with discreet icons planned for Pro after native acceptance. Android supports preset launcher names; an iPhone custom Home Screen name uses a user-created Shortcut. The selected free-tier direction is 25 active items, excluding Recently removed; billing and count enforcement remain disabled in current testing. Both periods have the same benefits and no selected trial or cloud storage. The prepared 250 MiB import target still requires device acceptance; current customer imports remain at 25 MiB. Existing items remain readable, editable, restorable and exportable after expiry, refund or cancellation. Restore purchases restores your plan, not your vault files.

How do the Calculator and Notebook covers work?

These optional Pro covers are implemented in current source and still require native acceptance. Hold = for 1.5 seconds in Calculator, or hold the Notebook title for 1.5 seconds, then unlock the vault normally. Reset the original appearance for free in vault Settings. Expiry does not change your cover or delete everyday data. Everyday Notebook notes, tasks, plans and saved laps are visible without vault authentication, separate from private notes and excluded from vault backups. Save before leaving; Recently removed is recoverable, and permanent deletion requires confirmation. Use private vault notes for sensitive content.

Which everyday covers are prepared?

Seven Pro covers are implemented in source: Calculator, Notebook, Unit Converter, To-do List, Stopwatch, Daily Planner and QR Scanner. Native acceptance is pending. Saved tasks/plans/laps stay on this device, outside vault backups and private-item limits. Save edits explicitly; there is no cover-data export/recovery service. Planner has no reminders or calendar sync; device clock changes can affect Stopwatch. QR scanning starts only by choice, needs camera permission, keeps no history and never opens links automatically. Copy text deliberately; review a website before confirming it. Expiry retains the chosen cover and its data; resetting the original appearance stays free.
