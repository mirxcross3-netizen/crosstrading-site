ScanTray Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Cross Trading LLC

The short version
  • Your documents stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics of our own.
  • We don't run a server that stores anything about you.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; our subscription service sees only the store receipt and a random ID.

Who we are

ScanTray is a document scanner app for iPhone and Android made by Cross Trading LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Your documents stay on your phone

Scanning, text recognition (OCR), PDF, Word and Excel export, redaction and encryption all run on your phone. Your scans, photos, recognised text, document titles, folders and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. We do not receive, store or have access to any of them.

What we don't collect

ScanTray has no account and no sign-in. It shows no ads, contains no advertising or tracking code, and we run no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or device identifiers, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan documents while you are scanning.
  • Photos — importing a photo uses the system photo picker, so ScanTray only sees the pictures you pick, never your whole library. Saving a page to Photos only adds that picture.
  • Face ID / fingerprint — handled by your phone. ScanTray is only told whether the check passed and never sees your biometric data.
  • Local network (iPhone) — used only when you choose "Send to computer".

Locked documents and the app passcode

Locked documents are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your password. We never see the password and cannot recover a locked document if it is lost. The app passcode is stored only as a salted hash on your phone.

Backups

  • iPhone: ScanTray's encrypted library is included in your phone's iCloud or computer backup. Those backups are made and kept by Apple under your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Android: ScanTray is left out of Android's automatic backup.
  • Backup files: you can make an encrypted ScanTray backup file and choose where to save it. It is protected by a passphrase you choose, which we never see.

When ScanTray uses the internet

ScanTray works offline. Apart from the ML Kit diagnostics described below, it connects to the internet only when you use one of these features:

  • Translation — the first time you translate into or from a language, the app downloads that language's model from Google. After that, translation runs on your phone and your text is not sent anywhere.
  • Import from web — the app downloads the web address you type, with the pictures and styles that page uses, from that website.
  • Send to computer — the file goes directly from your phone to a computer on the same Wi-Fi network. No internet server is involved.
  • Share, email and print — these open your phone's share sheet, and the file goes to the app or printer you choose, under that app's own policy.
  • Subscriptions — see below.

Google ML Kit

ScanTray uses Google's ML Kit libraries, which run on your phone, for translation, and on Android also for the document scanner (provided by Google Play services), text recognition and barcode scanning. These libraries send Google limited diagnostics and usage statistics, such as the phone model and system version, the app version, a random per-installation ID that does not identify you or your phone, which ML Kit features ran, how long they took, error codes, and for translation the two languages chosen. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics of ML Kit; for Android, Google states it is sent encrypted and not passed to third parties. It never includes your documents, images or text. See Google’s Android and iOS ML Kit data disclosures and Google’s privacy policy.

Subscriptions

ScanTray can be used for free; ScanTray Pro is an optional subscription. Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play and handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random anonymous ID created by the app. It never receives your documents, name or email address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document moves it to the app's Trash; emptying the Trash removes it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. Copies may remain in phone backups or backup files you made until you delete those. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, but you are always welcome to contact us.

Children

ScanTray is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights (including GDPR, UK GDPR and California)

We do not sell or share personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising and do not profile you. Because we do not collect or hold personal information about you, requests to access, correct, delete or move it have nothing to act on. We will still answer any question you send. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change means the app starts handling your information differently, we will say so in the app before it applies.
