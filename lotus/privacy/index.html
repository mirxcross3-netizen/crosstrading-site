Privacy Policy — Women's Tracker

Effective October 16, 2026

Your log lives on your device

Your cycle, symptom, food, mood, energy, sleep, medication and intimacy entries are stored in a local database on this phone, with the details you add to them: headache start and end times, where it hurts on the body map, self-care steps such as heat or rest, your “Did it help?” answers, and your monthly check-in answers about symptoms and how much they limited you. Weight can be logged only after you turn on Track weight in More → Cycle settings & units, which is off unless you turn it on; turning it off keeps weights already logged. Your app settings are stored there too, such as which reminders, check-ins and sections are on, whether the cycle-change card is on, and whether you have finished setup and your first log. When you turn on backup, a copy of your log and your settings (except the country you chose for support lines, which stays on this phone) is encrypted on your phone with a key protected by a recovery code that the app makes and only you hold (older backups use the passphrase you chose), and only that encrypted copy is stored with our backup provider (Supabase). We cannot read it and cannot reset the code. If you choose “Email it to me”, the code is sent from your own mail app to your own address, not through our servers. Until you turn on backup, nothing from your log leaves the phone. If you use your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup), the phone may include the app’s data in it, following your device settings.

Sensitive entries

The question about thoughts of self-harm is off unless you switch it on in More → Mood and safety. Your answers and your safety plan stay in your log on this phone, and in your encrypted backup if backup is on. They are never used by insights, Chat or the on-device AI. They appear in a doctor report only if you tick that section for that report and, on a phone with a passcode, unlock with Face ID, fingerprint or the passcode. What you log about alcohol, cannabis, nicotine, night shifts and travel, your reproductive history and the conditions you declare is kept the same way: on this phone, and in your encrypted backup only if you turn it on. Pain with sex, whether you note it on an intimacy entry, as a symptom or in a condition check-in, is kept the same way, and partner sharing never includes it. A condition is only ever what you say; the app never works one out from your log. Your reproductive history is in a doctor report only when you switch that section on.

Moving your history in

Someone helping you move from another app can leave a history file waiting in your account. When you sign in, your phone makes a key pair and shares only the public half with our backup provider (Supabase); the history is encrypted to that key (older imports use a one-time code instead), so the provider stores only an encrypted copy that only your phone can open. Nothing is added until you review it and confirm, it never overwrites a day you already logged unless the import says it corrects period days and you confirm that, and you can delete it from the account at any time.

Your account

You can use the app without an account: choose “Continue without an account” on the sign-in screen and everything stays on this phone. You can sign in later from More → Account & backup. An account (email, Apple or Google) is needed only for encrypted backup, partner sharing and history files left waiting for you. If you make one, Supabase stores your email address, how you sign in (email, Apple or Google) and when you signed in, so it can keep you signed in and let you reset your password. This is used only to run your account and backup, and is not linked to anything you log. If you sign in with Apple, Supabase also keeps a token from Apple for your account, used only to cancel the Sign in with Apple link when you delete the account; it is deleted with the account. Product-news emails are off unless you tick “Email me product news” when you create the account; that choice is stored with your account, and every such email has an unsubscribe link. You can delete your account and its backup at any time in More → Account & backup → Delete account and backup, even without your recovery code. Your log stays on this phone and the app keeps working without an account. Signing out stops partner sharing, and signing out or deleting the account removes the app’s keys for backup, partner sharing and imports from this phone.

Partner sharing

Partner sharing is off unless you turn it on in More → Partner sharing. It shares only today’s cycle phase, whether you’re on your period, when your next period may start, and period days from the last and next five weeks — never symptoms, food, mood, sex, notes, medications or your life stage. That view is encrypted on your phone with a key that exists only in the link you share, and our backup provider (Supabase) stores only the encrypted copy. Anyone with the link can see it. Stopping sharing deletes the shared copy, and the link stops working.

No tracking or analytics

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or any third-party SDK that tracks you. It makes network requests for five things only: checking whether a newer version of the app is available when you open it, signing in, uploading or downloading your encrypted backup, downloading an encrypted history file waiting in your account, and, if you turn it on, uploading the encrypted partner-sharing copy. The update check goes to our app-update provider (Expo) and sends nothing from your log. When the app checks for updates, Expo receives the app version, your phone’s platform (iPhone or Android) and a random ID for this install, plus a short technical error message if a downloaded update failed to start. It isn’t linked to your account or anything you log. Your encrypted backup is uploaded only when you turn backup on.

Insights and AI

Patterns, forecasts and trends are worked out on your phone from your own log. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the app can also use Apple’s on-device model for four things: rewording patterns that passed its statistical checks and answers in Chat, turning what you type in your own words into a draft entry, suggesting foods from a meal photo you choose, and picking which part of your log answers a question in Ask. That model runs on the phone, so your data is not sent to Apple, to us or to any AI company. A meal photo is looked at on the phone and the app’s copy is deleted afterwards. All four are off unless you turn them on in More → Insights & AI, and AI-written text is labeled. The app does not use any cloud AI service. We don’t use your personal data to train AI models.

Health Connect (Android)

On Android you can choose to connect Health Connect in More → Data & privacy. It is off unless you connect it, and the app works fully without it. The app then reads, on your phone, only your steps, workout time, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, basal body temperature and period days, and saves them in your log. It never writes anything to Health Connect, never reads weight, calories or food, and sends none of this anywhere; like the rest of your log, it is in your encrypted backup only if you turn backup on. You can disconnect at any time, in the app or in Health Connect’s settings.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Enabling one asks for the notification permission; nothing about your reminders leaves the phone. With App lock or discreet mode on, reminders show only “Reminder” on the lock screen. “Did it help?” follow-ups are off unless you turn them on in More → Reminders. When they are on, a few hours after you log an as-needed medicine or a self-care step such as heat or rest, one notification asks “Did it help?”. It never names the medicine or step, even with App lock and discreet mode off, and the notification carries only a reference to that entry, not what you took. Turning follow-ups off cancels any not yet delivered. If you add the Android home-screen widget, it shows your cycle day and next period on your home screen; with App lock or discreet mode on it shows only “Open to see today”.

App lock

The optional app lock uses your device’s own Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode prompt. The app never sees or stores your biometric data — it only learns whether the system prompt succeeded.

Your data, your choice

From More → Data & privacy you can export every entry as a JSON file to keep or move elsewhere, and you can delete all data. Deleting removes the database from this device immediately and cannot be undone. If you use cloud backup, your encrypted backup is kept and this phone stops backing up until you restore it or choose to replace it; you can delete the backup itself in More → Account & backup. Uninstalling the app removes your log from the phone.

Not medical advice

Predictions and insights are estimates computed from what you log. They are for personal awareness and are not a diagnosis, contraception method or substitute for care from a doctor or nurse.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and the date shown is updated. Questions: contact@crosstrading.llc.
