Delete your account

Women's Tracker. This page is linked from the app's store listing.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to More → Account & backup.
  2. Tap Delete account and backup and confirm.

This works even if you've lost your recovery code.

Without the app

Email contact@crosstrading.llc from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in method and sign-in times.
  • Your encrypted backup. We can't read it, and it can't be recovered once deleted.
  • Your encrypted partner-sharing copy, if you shared one. The link stops working.
  • Any encrypted history files still waiting in your account to be moved in.
  • The public key your phone stored with your account for those history files.
  • Your product-news email choice.

When you delete in the app, the app also removes its keys for backup, partner sharing and imports from that phone.

Our backup provider (Supabase) may keep its own backups of its servers to recover from failures. A deleted account can remain in those until they expire, for up to 7 days. Your health data in them is still the encrypted copy, which we can't read. Nothing else is kept. The log on your phone is not touched: it lives on the phone, and deleting the app removes it.
